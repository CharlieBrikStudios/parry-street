Built better

We finish to a standard significantly higher than typical Serco houses. Serco is desperate for good-quality homes, so ours get renewed.

Rent rises

Serco is known for regular rent rises, so it's very likely the rent rises through the lease. Both of these are already due one.

Fresh 7 years
